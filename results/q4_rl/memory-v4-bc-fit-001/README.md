# BC256 fixed teacher-input diagnostic

This is supervised classification on preselected **already trained synthetic TRAIN inputs**, not a new rollout, counterfactual simulation, independent validation, or promotion decision. Each job uses its immutable final BC256 `warmstart.pt` and complete BC batches **1 and 16**, selected before reading their losses. All four checkpoints pass their original strict G1/G3 loaders and have 256 completed BC episodes, 16 batches, zero PPO batches, and no pending batch.

The common panel contains 32 scenes (seeds 8012000–8012015 and 8012240–8012255), 11,613 teacher decisions: cover 8,980; localize 742; certified safe clear 339; grid clear 1,552. The report's `batch:1` and `batch:2` denote supplied-file order, corresponding to original BC batches 1 and 16 respectively.

Correcting top-1 for exactly identical float32 candidate features leaves each value unchanged. However, 58.07% of decisions have a teacher-equivalent alternative; their mean exact-feature CE floor is 1.0595. Equivalent-class probability is 75.58%, 73.30%, 77.27%, and 77.29% respectively. Raw CE and exact-label probability therefore also reflect unavoidable ambiguity among indistinguishable candidates; localize/grid actions have no such ambiguity on this panel.

The remaining localization mismatch is substantial (28.84–45.69% top-1). h128 improves this role over the corresponding h64 on the same panel, while other roles trade off. G3 h128 has the strongest overall top-1, G1 h128 the lowest CE and strongest localization top-1; neither dominates all categories. These checks support further investigation of imitation fit and representation, but do not establish better autonomous decisions. First-versus-sixteenth-batch results do not show a uniform recency advantage; two different scene batches cannot establish absence of forgetting. Comparisons against the old BC64 diagnostic are not causal because its scenes differ.

For physical context only, the **original teacher trajectories** clear 32/32 scenes, contain 2,064 failed clear attempts, average 8,196.8921 billed virtual seconds against mean common lower bound 1,755.4276 seconds, and have sum(T)/sum(L) = **4.669456**. These are not the evaluated frozen policies' rollout results. No truth, lower bound, seed, or evaluation outcome enters the policy tensors. No confidence interval or independent efficacy claim is inferred from correlated training decisions.
