# Memory and capacity experiment

The completed 64-episode BC initializer fits localization and optical-grid teacher labels poorly. A fixed-teacher-input diagnostic found top-1 agreement of 7.97% and 0.85%, versus 84.66% for coverage scans. This is supervised TRAIN-input evidence, not counterfactual policy evaluation. In the independent paired development run, excess scans and failure to finish discovery before the 512-decision handoff dominated the weak micro policy.

Predeclare four fresh runs: original G1 and negative-memory G3, each at hidden width 64 and 128. All use 256 BC episodes followed by PPO, fresh synthetic training seeds 8012000..8021999, RNG424444, unchanged public action candidates, single-request semantics, physical certificates, full undiscounted billed/fallback/failure objective, three update epochs and 512-decision horizon. No cross-schema initialization, training database or held-out scenarios are used. The new G3 appends eight finite negative-compatibility features; it does not add proof or skip permissions.

Each run has eight rollout workers plus one learner and 3600 wall seconds, under a single common CPU affinity pool. The four jobs declare 36 compute processes; the concurrent 6-worker paired evaluator plus parent adds seven. All numerical libraries use one thread, all jobs are CPU-only, and the total pool is at most 50 cores. Slower models may see fewer scenes and updates. Report actual BC completion, PPO batches, total episodes, measured CPU and wall time rather than assuming equal training work.

Both the BC endpoint and last complete PPO checkpoint will be frozen. No best-checkpoint selection. Preserve pending and rolled-back batches. Compare G1/G3 within width to assess the memory representation, widths within representation to assess capacity, and BC/PPO endpoints to assess training contribution. Any comparison against earlier 64-episode BC also changes the training curriculum and budget and must be labeled accordingly. Fixed paired development requests support comparison; fresh independent confirmation remains necessary before promotion. Every policy performance table includes frozen best baseline R8 actual time and the unchanged common lower-bound ratio.

Progress reviews occur approximately every 30 minutes. This is a bounded experiment, not a claim of convergence or optimality.
