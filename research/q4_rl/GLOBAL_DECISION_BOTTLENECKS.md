Read-only audit of frozen C commit `57e02aacc933ef600df588ab81141399354261e6`. This note changes no running/planned GAE or C evaluation. These are open directions if improvements plateau, not performance evidence or a theoretical-optimum claim. No new tests, scenarios, simulator or server calls were used.

**Current constraint.** `src/q4_rl/controller.py::_refresh_certificate/_terminal_gate` ends discovery after 16 distinct channels are actually observed; clearing still requires successful receipts. Below 16, the true count is unknown: finding ten does not imply completion. Every remaining unknown channel needs its own actual-negative absence certificate. G1/G3 credit only exact `compact_22` station measurements toward this condition. Public negatives elsewhere cannot retire fixed obligations, so better ordering or memory alone cannot express every useful omission.

**Absence versus plan redundancy.** For actual negative points A, complete directional coverage requires every source position x in the radius-1800 arena to belong to `conv({p in A: distance(p,x) <= 1000})`. This handles every half-plane orientation at minimum reception radius, hence also larger radii and omnidirectional sources. The existing checker certifies whole cells and the complete partition. C uses conservative floating margins; the separate adaptive-cover module adds exact rational witness checks on submitted coordinates. Neither is a license to ignore arithmetic assumptions or unresolved cells.

| Evidence | Permitted conclusion |
|---|---|
| Actual A alone has complete coverage | This channel is absent; its remaining scans may be suppressed. |
| A plus retained future points R, excluding p, has complete coverage | `(p, channel)` is no longer mandatory; the remaining discovery plan is feasible. This proves neither current absence nor that p has no information value. |
| G3's finite bank has zero compatible/visible rows | Approximate feature only; no absence, redundancy or safety certificate. |

Deletion checks must use updated sets or one atomic transaction: individually removable points need not be jointly removable. A whole station visit disappears only after all owed channels are handled and no retained service action still visits it. Measurements remain channel-specific. A single `no_signal` is not a 1000 m exclusion disk; even within 5 m a source can face away. Guaranteed uninformative measurements require every continuously feasible public-history hypothesis to predict the same reply. Geometric feasibility alone also does not guarantee completion within remaining action/time budgets.

**Representation/action limits.** G1 retains actual coordinates, positive geometry and grid history, but exposes aggregate coverage counts, geometric summaries and first/latest positive anchors. G3 appends eight negative-only finite-library scores without changing candidates or certificates. The actor lacks the full channel-position obligation relation and residual proof supports; mean/max candidate pooling cannot reconstruct omitted relationships. Unknown-channel actions are restricted to fixed stations and the current point. There is no certified replacement action. Internal channel grouping is valid; identity embeddings, case IDs, seeds and truth are not policy inputs.

**Three bounded future ablations:**

1. **Same candidates, residual-proof interface.** Compare fixed obligations with an exact ledger crediting real nonstation negatives and retiring proved-redundant obligations. Separate rule changes from retraining. Report actual skipped requests/visits, extra scans, proof CPU and complete paired T/L, not just deleted ledger entries.
2. **Shared-visit geometry and query timing.** Filter service-path replacements by public coverage-support deficits; reserve proof queries across observation stages. Include every added scan/switch in the schedule proxy, and preserve reply-by-reply interruption plus service insertion. The existing shared smoke accepted no replacement and spent its query allowance early; later useful geometry remains untested. Compare the old rule, revised proposals/query allocation, then learning.
3. **Relational public state.** Hold actions/certificates fixed while comparing aggregate features against grouped negative points, remaining-position masks and proof deficits, using coordinates and permutation-equivariant grouping. First distinguish equal-count histories with different obligations, then test complete rollout costs. Compatibility scores remain ranking features, never certificate masks.
